# Music

The music section is the heart of **Debrid Vault**. Manage your library, discover new music with personal recommendations, create custom playlists, and track your listening stats—all powered by your Debrid provider.

### Search
Find music by artist, album, or song. Results adapt based on your configured sources.

<img src="/img/debrid-vault/setup-guide/music/image-1766939058536.png" width="100%" />

:::note
**TorBox Pro Users:** You can utilize Usenet (via Newznab indexers like DrunkenSlug) to find rare content. If a song is missing, use the dropdown to switch to **YouTube Music** or **SoundCloud** via the Web Download feature to add any track directly to your cloud.
:::

### Library Management
View all music-related media stored in your Debrid provider. Play in order, shuffle, or manage your recent downloads.

* **Direct Import:** Copy hashes/magnet links or use original URLs for Web/Usenet downloads directly within DV.
* **Multi-select Actions:** Batch add songs to playlists, queues, or download them for local offline use.

<img src="/img/debrid-vault/setup-guide/music/importdvdebrid.png" width="100%" />

#### Filters & Metadata
Quickly sort by name, format, or cache expiry. DV automatically scrapes high-quality album art and metadata from Spotify, iTunes, MusicBrainz, and Deezer.
