## Social Tab
The Social Tab aggregates listening history across the entire community to showcase **Top Tracks, Artists, and Albums**. 

* **Community Curation:** Browse, like, and import playlists shared by other members.
* **Universal Support:** Works seamlessly with both **Debrid** and **Tidal** sources.
* **Leaderboards:** See which community members have the most liked playlists!

## Discovery Features
*Find your next favorite song with AI-powered curation.*

### Smart Shuffle & AI DJ (Gemini)
* **Smart Shuffle:** Tap shuffle **twice** to inject similar tracks into your queue. Look for the ✨ sparkle icon!
* **AI DJ:** Powered by the **Gemini API** to analyze your history and spin personalized sets of new music and deep cuts.

## Stream Mode & Tidal
Access **MP3 and FLAC/Lossless** streaming effortlessly. Eclipse merges your local and cloud libraries into one unified ecosystem.

* **Quality Control:** Independent quality settings for Cellular vs. WiFi.
* **Global Search:** Search across your library and **Tidal** simultaneously.
