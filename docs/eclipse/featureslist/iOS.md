# **iOS FEATURES LIST**

### **Jams**
Invite users to a Jam and listen to music together synced in real time (just like spotify jam),

---

### **Playlist/ Track Sharing**
* Share your playlists with users. Supports Debrid and stream mode songs!
* Debrid songs will get auto cached to users dashboard

### **Social Tab**
* Share playlists with other users on eclipse and see what the most popular songs, artists and albums are in eclipse eco system
* Playlists shared in Social tabs can be imported as your own playlist

### **Cloud Storage**
* Supports conection of webDAV, Plex and JellyFin

---

### **Customize UI**
* You are able to change color theme of your UI
* You can adjust how the seek bar looks like in fullscreen player
* Supports custom easter egg seek bars (play a star wars song for example)

### **Lyrics**
* Supports different animation of lyrics
* Real time translation of lyrics
* Different lyric providers that can be re arranged in settings

---

### **Eclipse Radio**
* On Home Tab you will notice different options of radio stations
* If you visit an artist, you can play that artists radio which will then queue songs from their discography

:::info AI & Discovery
**DJ AI** A working DJ AI that will know your listening history and habits and will provide recs and queue based on that

**Smart Shuffle** Similiar to Spotify, if you enable smart shuffle, every 2-3 songs will be auto queued by it adding more diversity to your session
:::

---

### **Animated Art**
* In settings you can enable animated art for Apple music, tidal and Debrid based songs (Though for debrid songs you have to apply them manually) 

### **Offline Support**
* Download songs for offline listening

### **Library**
* Can add songs to your library and playlists and its all part of same ecosystem
