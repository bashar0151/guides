The music section is the heart of **Debrid Vault**. Manage your library, discover new music with personal recommendations, create custom playlists, and track your listening stats—all powered by your Debrid provider.

## Core Playback Tools

- **Lyrics:** Real-time sync with manual offset, AI Translation, and animations. Features a priority-based fallback system.
- **Jellyfin Integration:** Seamlessly connect and stream from your Jellyfin libraries.
- **Playback Controls:** Adjustable playback speed and **Auto-Skip Silence** for a tighter experience.
- **Crossfade:** Gapless transitions between tracks.
- **Native Shazam Search:** Identify music on the go directly within Eclipse and search instantly.
- **Last.fm:** Native scrobbling support to track your listening habits.
- **Audio:** Professional EQ and Loudness Normalization.
- **Offline:** High-quality local playback without needing data.

### Library Management
View all music-related media stored in your Debrid provider. Play in order, shuffle, or manage your recent downloads.

* **Direct Import:** Copy hashes/magnet links or use original URLs for Web/Usenet downloads directly within DV.
* **Multi-select Actions:** Batch add songs to playlists, queues, or download them for local offline use.
