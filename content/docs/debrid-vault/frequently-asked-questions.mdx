*Commonly asked questions about Debrid Vault (DV).*

---

### **Q: What is Debrid Vault?**
> **Debrid Vault (DV)** is a feature-rich media manager that integrates with multiple debrid cloud providers like **Real-Debrid** and **TorBox**. It acts as a central hub to search, stream, and organise your entire library (Movies, Shows, Music, Books, and Games) in one place. You can search and add songs to the cloud, and also for example listen to music right in your browser.

### **Q: Which Debrid providers are supported?**
> Currently, Debrid Vault supports **TorBox** and **Real-Debrid**.

### **Q: Is there an iOS or Android app?**
> **No, Debrid Vault is only a web media manager for managing your debrid provider (TorBox or Real-Debrid).** If you're looking for an app experience, you might be interested in [Eclipse](/eclipse), which is a separate app from Debrid Vault.

### **Q: How do I get the most out of DV?**
> Check out the official **Tips and Tricks for DV** in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/A2KrtxH653) for basic guides. You can also ask questions directly in the **Eclipse + Debrid Vault Discord** or by exploring this **Unofficial Wiki**!

### **Q: Where do I get help or report a bug?**
> 1. [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/A2KrtxH653). The invite takes you to the [roles channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1435676615962263781).
> 2. Pick your provider role (**Real Debrid** or **TorBox**) to unlock the support channels.
> 3. Ask for help there, or report bugs in the [bug reports channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1495982765227901072).
