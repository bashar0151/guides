The Eclipse homepage is your central hub—giving you instant access to your playlists, quick playback tools, and personalized listening statistics.

---

## Homepage Overview
- **Customizable Layout:** Personalize and rearrange your homepage sections to surface what you listen to most.
- **Quick Access & Pins:** Jump straight into your created playlists, pinned folders, or connected cloud libraries.
- **Instant Playback:** Use **Quick Mix** to shuffle your library immediately or launch **DJ AI (Umbra)** for a tailored session.

---

## Listening Stats & Yearly Replay
Track your listening habits across custom timeframes or look back at your full year in music:

- **Top Tracks & Artists:** View your top 5 most-listened tracks in ranking order alongside your top artists over your chosen time period.
- **Yearly Replay:** Explore your annual listening stats and year-in-review summary built from everything you play across your synced devices.
