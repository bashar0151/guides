Moving over from another streaming platform? You can import your existing playlists and liked tracks directly into Eclipse—and keep them automatically synced whenever you add new music.

**Currently Supported Services:**
* **Spotify**
* **YouTube Music**

---

## How to Import & Sync Your Music

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Linked Services">
    In Eclipse, go to **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Linked services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Streaming Account">
    Select your streaming provider (**Spotify** or **YouTube Music**) and sign in to link your account.
    *Verification:* Your account name appears under the service header along with a timestamp showing when it was last synced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger a Manual Sync (Optional)">
    Tap **Sync now** at any time to immediately pull in newly added playlists or liked tracks, or tap **Disconnect** to unlink your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Sync Settings & Controls

Once connected, you can customize what gets imported and how your library stays updated:

### **Synced Playlists**
* Tap **Show** next to **Synced playlists** to choose which individual playlists are imported and continue receiving newly added tracks.

### **Add Liked Songs to Favorites**
* Toggle on **Add liked songs to Favorites** to automatically mirror tracks you like on your streaming service into your **Eclipse Favorites**.
* **Safe Sync Behavior:** If you unlike a track on your linked streaming account, it is also removed from your Eclipse Favorites—however, tracks you favorited natively inside Eclipse are **never removed**.
* **Library Cap:** For large libraries, Eclipse imports up to the **newest 5,000 liked songs**.

### **Use Linked Services in Umbra DJ**
* Enable **Use linked services in Umbra DJ** to share your top artists with your configured AI provider for more personalized mixes and recommendations.
