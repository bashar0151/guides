## Cross-Device Sync & Casting

### Account & Preference Sync
Sign in with your **Eclipse Account** to automatically sync your app preferences, settings, addons, and library across all your logged-in devices.

### Cross-Session Playback, Connect & Handover
- **Cross-Session Playback & Connect:** Control any of your active devices from another, or pick up playback right where you left off across desktop, mobile, and web.
- **Handover Mode:** Instantly transfer your current playback and queue between your Mac and iPhone.

### Chromecast & Speakers
Stream your music wirelessly with **Chromecast support** (available on Android) and easy connections to external speakers.

## Apple Ecosystem (iOS, iPadOS, macOS & CarPlay)
- **iPhone, iPad & Mac:** Dedicated native apps with full library sync, offline downloads, and desktop enhancements on macOS.
- **Siri Integration:** Control playback hands-free across your Apple devices—for example: *"Hey Siri, play Driving playlist on Eclipse."*
- **CarPlay:** Take your music on the road with native dashboard controls, Siri voice commands, and a simplified driving UI.

---

## Windows & Web Player
- **Windows:** Native Windows 11 desktop player featuring full-screen skins, Eclipse Connect, Jams, DJ AI, music videos, and automatic self-updates.
- **Web Player:** Run Eclipse in any modern browser with zero installation—includes a big-target **Drive Mode** for in-car browser use.

---

## Android, Android Auto & TV
- **Android (Phone & Tablet):** Open beta APK available at [eclipsemusic.app/download](https://eclipsemusic.app/download) (Android 8.0+), featuring Chromecast, offline downloads, a 10-band EQ, and the full DSP suite.
- **Android Auto:** Native dashboard controls and a simplified UI for safe driving.
- **Android TV, Google TV & Fire TV:** Dedicated TV APK available via direct download or Downloader code `1267304`.

## Platform Support & Bug Reports

Every platform has its own support channel on Discord:

1. [Join the Discord server](https://discord.gg/A2KrtxH653). The invite takes you to the [roles channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1435676615962263781).
2. Pick your platform role: **iOS**, **MacOS**, **tvOS**, **Android**, **Android TV**, **Windows** or **Web Player**.
3. Ask for help in your platform's channel, or report bugs in the [bug reports channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1495982765227901072).
