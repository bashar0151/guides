Add-ons are where your music catalogs come from. They live on your account, so every Eclipse app you sign in to automatically syncs and gets the exact same sources.

<Callout type="info" title="Need support for an add-on?">
1. [Join the Discord server](https://discord.gg/A2KrtxH653). The invite takes you to the [roles channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1435676615962263781).
2. Select the role for the add-on you need help with. This unlocks its support channel.
3. Use the **Support** links in the tables below to jump straight to it.

The Support links only work once you've joined the server and picked the matching role.
</Callout>

## Add-on Architecture & Store
Using a flexible manifest-style system, Eclipse lets you pull source catalogs directly into your application. 

- **Built-in Add-on Store:** Browse and discover available music sources directly inside the app's **Add-on Store**.
- **App & Web Management:** Manage, configure, and reorder your add-ons either directly in the Eclipse app or via your web account dashboard. Switch between **Simple** or **Advanced** modes to customize priority chains for primary and secondary data.

### Manual Installation Instructions
To install custom or third-party add-ons via manifest URL:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the App
    Launch the Eclipse application on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Navigate to Settings
    Open **Settings** by tapping the cog wheel icon.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Access Music Sources
    Select **Music Sources** from the menu options.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open Connections
    Tap **Connections**, then select **Add Connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Select Cloud
    Choose **Cloud** as your connection type.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Paste and Connect
    Paste the manifest URL from your chosen add-on source and hit **Connect** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Important Note on TorBox Airlocking (Aero, Nebula, & Nullbeat)">
You might hesitate to use airlocking or clear files out of fear that it will break your setup, but please keep the following in mind:

* **Storage Capacity:** Even on an Essential plan with lossless tracks, it takes roughly **10,000 different tracks** to fill your airlock storage (scaling up to 17k on Standard and 34k on Pro).
* **What happens when full?** Nothing breaks. No warnings or errors appear. TorBox and your add-ons will continue to function normally. The only restriction is that new airlocks are blocked, but you can still play new songs seamlessly. 
* **Our Recommendation:** Please do not delete airlocked tracks or clear files from your account. Deleting them makes your experience worse, not better!
</Callout>
