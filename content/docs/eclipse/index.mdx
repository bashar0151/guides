**Eclipse** is a cross-platform music player built for your own collection across **Windows, Android, Android TV, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Web**. Whether your tracks live in your cloud library, local folders on your device, or community add-ons, Eclipse brings everything together into one unified library with synced preferences across every device.

Head over to [eclipsemusic.app/download](https://eclipsemusic.app/download) to install the app on your device or launch the browser player immediately. Once installed, follow the **[Setup Guide](/eclipse/setup-guide)** to connect your add-ons, cloud storage, and music accounts.

---

### Supported Platforms

| Platform | App Type | Key Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Windows & Mac** | Desktop App | Local folder scanning, full library & preference sync, Eclipse Connect, Handover |
| **Android** | Mobile App | 10-band EQ & full DSP suite, Chromecast, offline downloads, native **Android Auto** |
| **iPhone & iPad** | Mobile App | Siri integration, custom app icons & skins, offline downloads, native **CarPlay** |
| **Android TV / Fire TV** | TV App | Big-screen living room UI via APK or Downloader code `1267304` |
| **Web** | Browser Player | Zero install required, includes big-target **Drive Mode** for the car |
