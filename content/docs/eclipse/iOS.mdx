---
title: "App Features"
---

A breakdown of the core features and interactive experiences available across all Eclipse apps on mobile and desktop.

---

### **Jams**
Invite friends to a live **Jam session** and listen to music together synced in real time across devices.

---

### **Playlist & Track Sharing**
- Share your playlists with other users. Supports both Debrid and streaming mode tracks.
- Debrid-linked songs automatically get cached to the receiving user's dashboard.

### **Social & Discovery Tab**
- Share and explore playlists across the Eclipse ecosystem, and see what the most popular songs, artists, and albums are right now.
- Instantly import community playlists shared in the Social tab directly into your own library.

### **Cloud Storage**
- Supports direct connections to **WebDAV, Plex, and Jellyfin**.
- **Music Videos:** Watch music videos right inside Eclipse by toggling the feature under `CLOUD STORAGE -> ADD CONNECTION -> CLOUD`.

---

### **Customization & UI**
- **Dynamic Themes:** Change the color theme of your UI to adapt to the currently playing song.
- **Player & Seek Bar Styles:** Customize how the seek bar looks in the fullscreen player, including custom easter egg seek bars (like playing a Star Wars track).

### **Lyrics**
- Customize lyric animation styles (such as Karaoke or Apple Music styles).
- Real-time **AI Translation** of lyrics for foreign-language music.
- Reorder and manage different lyric providers directly in your settings.

---

### **Eclipse Radio & AI**
- **Eclipse Radio:** Access different radio stations right from your home tab, or visit any artist profile to launch an artist radio that queues tracks from their discography.
- **DJ AI (Umbra):** A built-in AI DJ that analyzes your listening history and habits to recommend and queue tracks tailored to your taste.
- **Smart Shuffle:** Automatically queues fresh tracks every 2 to 3 songs to add more diversity and discovery to your listening session.

---

### **Animated Art**
- Enable animated album artwork in settings for Apple Music, Tidal, and Debrid-based songs (with manual application options for Debrid tracks).

### **Offline Support & Library**
- **Offline Downloads:** Download songs, albums, and playlists for offline listening.
- **Unified Library:** Add songs, cloud files, and playlists seamlessly into a single synced library across all your devices.
