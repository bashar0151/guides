Eclipse runs natively across your desktop, mobile, TV, and in-car displays—keeping your music library and active queue in sync wherever you listen.

---

## macOS Support

Eclipse features a dedicated macOS application built for desktop listening.

### Handover Mode
Seamlessly switch between your Mac and iPhone using **Handover Mode**, transferring your current playback and queue between devices instantly.

<Callout type="success" title="Handover Requirements">
To use Handover, ensure both your Mac and iPhone are signed into the same Eclipse account and have Bluetooth enabled.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Showcase Coming Soon">
We are currently working on a video demonstration of Handover Mode and will add it here soon.
</Callout>

## Mobile & TV Platforms

* **iOS & iPadOS:** Full native support available via the App Store and TestFlight.
* **Android Phones & Tablets:** Full APK support available directly from [eclipsemusic.app/download](https://eclipsemusic.app/download).
* **Android TV, Google TV & Fire TV:** Dedicated TV interface optimized for remote navigation. See the [Setup Guide](/eclipse/setup-guide) for Downloader codes and sideloading steps.
