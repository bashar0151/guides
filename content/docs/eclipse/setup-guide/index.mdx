---

## Prerequisites

Before setting up the app, make sure you have:
1. **Debrid Vault Account:** Registered and active.
2. **Debrid Vault API Key:** Created via your Debrid Vault account under **Settings > Accounts > Mobile App Access**

---

## Step 1: Install Eclipse

Choose your platform below to get the app installed:

### **iOS (iPhone, iPad, Mac)**
<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the App">
    Access Eclipse via the official **App Store** or **TestFlight** invite links at [eclipsemusic.app/download](https://eclipsemusic.app/download).
    *Verification:* TestFlight or the App Store shows the download option for Eclipse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Install the app on your device and sign in with your Eclipse account credentials.
    *Verification:* You arrive at the main Eclipse home interface.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Android Phones & Tablets**
<Steps>
  <Step title="Download APK">
    Download the latest Android APK directly from [eclipsemusic.app/download](https://eclipsemusic.app/download).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Unknown Sources">
    Ensure your device allows [**"Install from unknown sources"**](/eclipse/setup-guide/install-app-from-unknown-sources-on-android-device) in your Android security settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and Open">
    Run the downloaded APK to install Eclipse, then open the app and sign in.
    *Verification:* The app launches successfully and allows sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Android TV, Google TV & Fire TV**
<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Downloader">
    Open the **Downloader** app on your TV platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Code">
    Enter Downloader code **`1267304`** to fetch and install the Eclipse TV APK.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant TV Permissions">
    Ensure your TV system settings allow the Downloader app to [**"Install from unknown sources"**](/eclipse/setup-guide/install-app-from-unknown-sources-on-android-device).
    *Verification:* The Eclipse TV app installs and appears in your television's app drawer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Link Add-ons & Music Sources

To stream music and pull catalogs into your library:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate Manifest URL">
    Visit the **Eclipse Add-ons** page to select your media sources, customize preferences, and generate your custom **Manifest URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open Eclipse on your device and tap the **Settings (gear) icon** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Connection">
    Navigate to **Cloud Storage** > **Add Connection** > **Cloud**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste and Connect">
    Paste your Manifest URL and hit **Connect**. 
    *Verification:* Your playlists and library sources sync and appear instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 3: Connect Cloud Servers & Personal Media

You can connect external storage and personal media servers using the same connection menu:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Cloud Storage">
    Go to **Settings** > **Cloud Storage** > **Add Connection** in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Server Type">
    Choose your server type from the options available—including **Plex, Jellyfin, WebDAV, or Google Drive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Sign in with your server credentials. 
    *Verification:* Once authenticated, your personal media appears directly under your library sources tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 4: Link Apple Music (Optional)

If you have an active Apple Music subscription, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Integrations">
    Open Eclipse and go to **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Apple Music**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Sign in with your Apple ID account credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Streaming">
    Enable streaming to enjoy your subscription tracks in **Lossless and Dolby Atmos quality** directly inside Eclipse.
    *Verification:* Apple Music tracks stream successfully with high-res options available.
  </Step>
</Steps>
