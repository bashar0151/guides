---
title: "Installing Apps from Unknown Sources on Android"
description: "Step-by-step instructions to enable sideloading and install APKs on Android Mobile, Android TV, and Android Auto."
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

By default, Android devices restrict the installation of apps from outside the official Google Play Store. To install Eclipse via APK or sideloaded sources, you need to enable **"Install from unknown sources"** depending on your device type.

---

## 1. Android Phones & Tablets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Android Settings">
    Open the **Settings** app on your phone or tablet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Security or Apps">
    Go to **Apps & Notifications** (or **Security & Privacy** depending on your Android version / manufacturer like Samsung or Google Pixel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Install Unknown Apps">
    Look for **Special app access** > **Install unknown apps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Permission to Your Browser / File Manager">
    Select the app you plan to use to download or open the APK (e.g., **Chrome**, **Firefox**, or your file manager) and toggle **Allow from this source** ON.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 2. Android TV, Google TV & Fire TV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Developer Options">
    Go to your TV **Settings** > **System** > **About**. Scroll down to **Android TV OS Build** (or Build Number) and click it **7 times** until it says "You are now a developer."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Developer Options">
    Go back one screen to **System** > **Developer options**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Unknown Apps">
    Scroll down and find the **Downloader** app (or your file browser app) in the list, then toggle permission to **Allow** or enable **Unknown Sources**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 3. Android Auto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Android Auto Settings">
    Open the **Android Auto** app on your connected mobile phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock Developer Mode">
    Scroll all the way down to the **Version** information block at the bottom and tap it **10 times** rapidly until a notification pops up confirming that developer settings have been enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Developer Settings">
    Tap the **three-dot menu icon** in the top-right corner of the Android Auto screen and select **Developer settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Unknown Sources">
    Scroll through the developer menu and check the box next to **Unknown sources**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

<Callout type="success" title="Ready to Install!">
Once enabled, you can successfully download, install, or sync your Eclipse APK across your Android devices without interruption.
</Callout>
