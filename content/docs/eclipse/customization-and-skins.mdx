Make Eclipse truly yours with comprehensive app skinning, dynamic themes, customizable player layouts, and audio profiles.

## App Skins & Dynamic Themes
Change how Eclipse looks—colors, fonts, **Liquid Glass** effects, backgrounds, custom images, tab bars, and mini players.

- **Dynamic Themes:** Automatically adapt the app's color palette to match the artwork of the currently playing song.
- **Mobile Skins:** Display on iPhone, iPad, and Android.
- **Desktop Skins:** Display on Mac, Windows, and the web player.
- Import community-shared skins or build your own.

---

## Homepage & Discovery Customization
Tailor your home and discovery feeds to show only what you want to see:

- **Customizable Homepage:** Rearrange and personalize your homepage layout to fit your listening habits.
- **Custom Catalogs & Visibility Toggle:** Customize your **Discovery** tab with custom catalogs, or directly show and hide sections using the **gear/cog icon** in the top-right corner.

---

## Player Styles & Player Studio
Switch between popular built-in player layouts or design your own fullscreen interface from scratch:

- **Built-in Player Styles:** Choose between **Apple Music**, **Spotify**, or custom player styles directly in your settings.
- **Animated Art & Video Toggle:** Switch seamlessly between animated album artwork and music videos right on the Now Playing screen.
- **Lyrics Styling:** Customize your lyrics display with **Karaoke** or **Apple Music** styles, plus real-time **AI Translation** for foreign-language songs.
- **Player Studio:** Phone players show on iPhone and Android; desktop players show on Windows, Mac, and the web player. All custom studio changes sync instantly to every device via [eclipsemusic.app/studio/skins](https://eclipsemusic.app/studio/skins).

---

## Eclipse Audio & Audio Profiles
Fine-tune your sound with a professional 10-band equalizer, advanced Android DSP suite, and custom audio profiles that travel with your account across every device.
