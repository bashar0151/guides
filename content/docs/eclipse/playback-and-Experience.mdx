Eclipse is designed for an immersive listening experience, blending high-tech AI discovery, live radio, and smart playback tools into a sleek interface.

---

## Eclipse Jam Sessions
Invite friends to join a **Jam session** and listen to music together synced in real-time across devices. Queue, skip, and pause tracks collaboratively.

---

## Eclipse Radio & Discovery Tab
Find your next favorite song or tune into live music channels:

- **Eclipse Radio:** Access live music channels, genre-based stations (like Chill), community-driven stations (Most Popular Music), and official Eclipse Radio stations right from your Home tab. Artist radio stations can also be launched directly from any artist profile to queue tracks from their discography.
- **Discovery Tab & Customization:** Explore new and popular tracks, customize your feeds with external catalogs, and use the **gear/cog icon** in the top-right corner to easily show or hide content sections.
- **Social Tab:** Aggregate listening history across the community to browse, like, and import shared playlists.

---

## AI DJ (Umbra) & Smart Shuffle
- **AI DJ (Umbra):** Powered by your API-linked AI model and taste weightings from your active platforms, Umbra analyzes your listening history and habits to recommend and automatically queue personalized sets.
- **Smart Shuffle:** Intelligent shuffle playback that intelligently injects similar tracks to bring more variety to your session.

---

## Podcasts & Audiobooks
Enjoy full, dedicated support for browsing and playing podcasts and audiobooks alongside your music library—complete with chapter lists, playback speed adjustments, and skip intervals.

---

## Visual Experience & Customization

- **Animated Album Art & Video Toggle:** Bring your library to life with motion. Toggle seamlessly between animated album artwork and music videos right on the Now Playing screen.
- **Dynamic Themes:** The UI automatically adapts its color palette to match the mood and artwork of the currently playing song.
- **Player Styles & Player Studio:** Choose between built-in player styles (like Apple Music or Spotify) or design your own custom fullscreen players using Player Studio. Changes sync automatically across devices via [eclipsemusic.app/studio/skins](https://eclipsemusic.app/studio/skins).
- **Easter Eggs:** Discover hidden UI themes triggered by specific music franchises.

---

## Core Playback Tools

- **Lyrics & AI Translation:** Customize lyric display styles (such as Karaoke or Apple Music style) with real-time **AI Translation** for foreign-language tracks and a priority-based fallback provider system.
- **Playback Controls:** Adjustable playback speed, Sleep Timer, Auto-Skip Silence, crossfade, and gapless transitions.
- **Audio & DSP Suite:** Professional 10-band equalizer, advanced Android DSP suite, Automix, and Loudness Normalization.
- **Integration & Utilities:** Native Shazam music identification, Last.fm scrobbling, and offline download support.
