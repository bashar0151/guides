---
title: "Library & Organisation"
---
Manage your music, podcasts, and collections with precision. This section covers how Eclipse helps you organize, tag, search, and share your unified library across devices.

---

## Playlists, Folders & Pins
Create and manage your playlists with custom names and high-resolution cover art.

- **Folders & Pins:** Organize your library your way—group playlists into folders, pin your favorites to the top for quick access, and fold away the rest.
- **Custom Catalogs:** Add and customize community or personal catalogs to surface curated collections directly in your library and Discovery views.

### Playlist Import & Streaming
Easily import your existing **Spotify, Apple Music, or YT Music** playlists directly into Eclipse. Link your Apple Music subscription to blend your streaming catalog seamlessly into your unified library.

### AI Prompt Playlist Generation
Create custom playlists using natural language AI prompts powered by your own API-linked AI model configured inside the Eclipse app settings.

---

## Search & Browse
- **Add-on Filtered Search:** Search across your music sources using the Liquid Glass search interface, complete with a dropdown selector to filter results by a specific add-on.
- **Browse Section:** Explore curated categories and catalogs directly underneath the search bar inside the Search tab.

---

## Cloud Storage, Local Files & Media Types
- **Local Music Scanning:** Point Eclipse at local folders on your desktop or audio files on your phone to import and index your personal collection.
- **Cloud Connections:** Connect and stream directly from **WebDAV, Plex, and Jellyfin**.
- **Podcasts & Audiobooks:** Full support for podcasts and audiobooks alongside your music library, automatically switching to dedicated controls like chapter lists, playback speed adjustment, and skip intervals.
- **Music Videos:** Watch music videos right inside Eclipse by toggling the feature under `CLOUD STORAGE -> ADD CONNECTION -> CLOUD`.

---

## Favorites, Quick Mix & Offline
- **Favorites:** Tap the heart icon on any track to add it to your Favorites.
- **Quick Mix:** Instantly play and shuffle tracks from your library with a single tap.
- **Offline Downloads:** Download songs, albums, and playlists for offline listening when you are on a plane or traveling.

---

## Metadata Management

### Edit & Extract Metadata
Manually search for metadata or pull tags (track names, high-res art) directly from your files.

### Auto Extract
Eclipse automatically handles new tracks as they are added, saving you the manual work.

---

## Multi-selection & Sharing
- **Multi-select:** Group, Favorite, or add multiple tracks to a playlist at once.
- **Share Links & Tracks:** Send a track, album, or playlist link that opens directly in Eclipse on any platform, or exchange files with fellow users for easy importing.
