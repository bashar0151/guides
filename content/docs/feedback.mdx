## Which option should I use?

| You want to... | Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| Fix a typo or an outdated step on one page | **Suggest an edit** link at the bottom of that page |
| Tell me a guide is confusing or wrong | **Suggest an edit** form above |
| Ask for a guide that doesn't exist yet | **Request a new guide** form above |
| Get help with an app or add-on itself | The [Discord](https://discord.gg/A2KrtxH653): pick your roles in the [roles channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1435676615962263781) to unlock the support channels |
| Report a bug in Eclipse or Debrid Vault | The [bug reports channel](https://discord.com/channels/1435540472360599606/1495982765227901072) |

<Callout type="info" title="This wiki is unofficial">
Feedback here is about the guides. Feature ideas and bug reports for Eclipse and Debrid Vault themselves go to the developer through the Discord, not through this page.
</Callout>
